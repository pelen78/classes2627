Stage 1
Desired Results
What do we want students to understand, know, and be able to do?
Established Goals / Standards
ISTE 1 — Empowered Learner Students set goals and reflect on their own learning process.
ISTE 6 — Creative Communicator Students communicate ideas using digital tools and platforms.
Google Apps Proficiency Competent use of Google Workspace tools for creation and collaboration.
Digital Literacy Understanding how to select and use digital tools appropriately.
Understandings & Questions
Enduring Understandings
  • The tools we choose to communicate reflect how we think and work digitally.
  • Knowing what you already know is the first step to learning something new.
  • Digital presentation is a skill — clarity and organization matter as much as content.
Essential Questions
  • How do I present myself effectively in a digital environment?
  • What do my tool choices say about how I work?
  • What digital skills do I already have — and where do I want to grow?
Knowledge & Skills
Students will Know…
  • What tools are available for digital content creation (Slides, Canva, Docs, etc.)
  • The basic elements of visual organization and readability
  • The expectations and grading criteria for Computer Applications
Students will be able to Do…
  • Select and use a digital tool independently to create a structured visual product
  • Present their work clearly to an audience within a time limit
  • Reflect honestly on their own digital skill level in writing
Stage 2
Assessment Evidence
How will we know if students have achieved the desired results?
Performance Task
Students create a Digital Identity Card using any tool of their choice. The card must include their name, 3 specific computer skills they already have, 1 tech skill they want to learn this year, something personal about themselves, and at least one meaningful image. Students present their card to the class (2 min max) and submit a written reflection on Friday.
Grading Criteria
Criterion Description Type
All 5 Required Elements Name, 3 skills, 1 goal, personal info, and at least one image — all present and complete Formative
Visual Clarity Information is easy to read and organized — not cluttered or hard to follow Formative
Oral Presentation Presented clearly to class within 2 minutes; student can answer a peer question Formative
Friday Reflection "What did you discover about your digital skills this week?" — honest, thoughtful answer submitted in Google Classroom before end of class Formative
Teacher Observation Notes (Diagnostic)
While students work Tuesday–Wednesday, observe and note: Which tools do students reach for naturally? Who experiments vs. who plays it safe? Who struggles with basic file management or layout? Who finishes early and goes beyond requirements? This informal data informs pacing and grouping for the rest of the semester.
Stage 3
Learning Plan
What sequence of teaching and learning experiences will get students there?
Mon Intro & Brainstorm
Activities
  • Course syllabus overview (10 min)
  • Class brainstorm: what tools do you know?
  • Assignment walkthrough — review instruction page
  • Students begin planning their card
Teacher Role
  • Facilitate brainstorm discussion
  • Clarify expectations and answer questions
  • Begin informal observation
Resources
  • Assignment instruction page
  • Google Classroom post
  • Projector / whiteboard
Tue Work Session 1
Activities
  • Students choose their tool and begin building
  • Include all 5 required elements
  • No teacher-directed instruction — independent work
Teacher Role
  • Circulate and observe only
  • Take diagnostic notes on tool choice and skill level
  • No hints or direct help
Resources
  • Classroom computers
  • Student's tool of choice
  • Teacher observation checklist
Wed Work Session 2
Activities
  • Continue and refine card
  • Check all 5 required elements are present
  • Prepare a 2-minute verbal presentation
Teacher Role
  • Continue observation
  • Remind students of presentation timing
  • Begin noting who may need extra time
Resources
  • Classroom computers
  • Assignment rubric on screen
Thu Presentations
Activities
  • Each student presents (2 min max)
  • Classmates ask 1 question per presenter
  • Submit card to Google Classroom
Teacher Role
  • Facilitate Q&A
  • Keep time per presenter
  • Record observations for grade
Resources
  • Projector / TV screen
  • Google Classroom submission
  • Timer
Fri Reflection
Activities
  • Written reflection in Google Classroom
  • Prompt: "What did you discover about your digital skills this week?"
  • Due before end of class
Teacher Role
  • Debrief week as a class (10 min)
  • Share what was observed across the group
  • Preview what's coming next unit
Resources
  • Google Classroom assignment
  • Reflection prompt on screen
