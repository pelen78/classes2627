Who Are You, Digitally?

Course Computer Applications
Grade 9th Grade
Duration Full Week
Teacher Ms. Montes

What's This About?

In this assignment you make a Digital Identity Card that introduces you to the class. Pick any app or software you're comfortable with. Your tool selection is part of the assignment.

The Week

What we'll do each day.

Monday
  • Course Syllabus
  • Class brainstorm: what tools do you know?
  • Assignment walkthrough
  • Start planning your card
Tuesday
  • Choose your tool
  • Start building your card
  • Include required content
Wednesday
  • Continue & refine
  • Make it visually clear
  • Check all 4 required elements
  • Prepare to present
Thursday
  • 2 min each presentation
  • Show your card on screen
  • Classmates ask 1 question
  • Submit to Google Classroom
Friday
  • Written reflection in GC
    • What did you discover about your digital skills this week?

Your card must include these 5 elements

All four are required. Everything else is your creative choice.

01
Your Name

How you like to be called.

02
3 Things You Know How to Do on a Computer

Be specific. "Use Google" doesn't count Ex: "build a Slides presentation with animations" does.

03
1 Tech Skill You Want to Learn This Year

Something from class, from life, or from your own curiosity.

04
Something Personal About You

A hobby, a fun fact, something that represents you

05
AImages

A photo, icon, or graphic that represents you. No random clipart, make it mean something.

Tool Choice: It's Up to You

Use any software or platform you already know. Your choice tells us something about how you work. Here are some options, but you're not limited to these:

Google Slides Google Docs Google Drawing Canva Something else entirely ✦
